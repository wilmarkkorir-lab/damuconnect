Built for Kenya.
Built to Save Lives.

DamuConnect is a technology platform connecting blood donors with patients in need — making the process fast, location-aware, and accessible to all Kenyans.

Our Mission

Kenya needs approximately 500,000 units of blood every year, but collects far less. The gap between donors and patients is not one of willingness — it is one of connection and communication.

DamuConnect exists to close that gap. We use SMS technology, location matching, and a simple, fast interface to connect people who want to donate with people who desperately need blood — right now, in their city.

We work with hospitals, clinics, and community health workers across all 47 counties to make blood accessible when and where it's needed most.

The Problems We Solve

🏥 Hospital Blood Shortages

Public hospitals like KNH face daily shortages. Patients in surgery, childbirth, and emergency care wait hours or die for lack of a matching donor nearby.

📢 Inefficient Communication

Families post desperate pleas on Facebook and WhatsApp groups. This reaches few people and takes too long. DamuConnect reaches the right donors instantly.

🗺️ Geography Barriers

Even when donors exist, they don't know where to go. Our location-matching algorithm finds donors within 10km of the hospital in seconds.
