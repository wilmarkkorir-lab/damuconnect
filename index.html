How It Works

Life-saving in three simple steps

From emergency to donation in under 30 minutes — powered by SMS and location matching.

01
🩸

Donor Registers

Share your blood type, location, and phone. That's all. You're now part of Kenya's life-saving network.

02
🏥

Hospital Posts Request

A doctor posts an emergency — blood type, urgency, hospital location. Takes under 60 seconds.

03
📱

You Get Alerted

If you're nearby and compatible, you receive an SMS or WhatsApp instantly. Show up. Donate. Save a life.
